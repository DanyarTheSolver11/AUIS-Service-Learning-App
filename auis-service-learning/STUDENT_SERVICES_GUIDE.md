# Student Services Guide — AUIS Volunteering Hours Tracker

This is a plain-language guide to running the app day-to-day. No technical
background needed. For anything outside this guide, contact IT.

## Signing in

Go to the app's website and click **Continue with AUIS Google Account**,
using your `@auis.edu.krd` email. You'll land on the **Registry** page —
that's your home base.

## The four tabs

- **Registry** — every student who has logged hours this semester, a
  quick stats summary, and your ceremony export.
- **Entries** — every individual submission, one at a time. Review,
  approve, reject, edit, delete, or step in on a specific entry.
- **Semesters** — open a new submission window each cycle.
- **Message Students** — send a one-off email to a filtered group of
  students, without leaving the app.

## Registry — your at-a-glance summary

At the top you'll see four numbers: how many students have logged
anything this semester, total confirmed hours across all of them, how
many entries are still waiting on a supervisor, and how many students
currently qualify for an award (10+ confirmed hours).

Below that, the full table — every engaged student, their hours, and
their tier — sorted highest to lowest.

Two export buttons:
- **Ceremony List (CSV)** — only students who qualify (10+ hours), sorted
  and ready for the ceremony.
- **All Entries (CSV)** — every individual submission regardless of
  status, useful if you ever need to double-check something or keep a
  full record.

## Entries — reviewing and stepping in

Most entries resolve themselves — the app emails the supervisor a
one-click confirm/reject link automatically. But you can:

- **Search** by student name/email or department, and **filter** by
  status (defaults to showing Pending first).
- **Approve** / **Reject** directly — for when a supervisor is
  unreachable, has left AUIS, or you've confirmed the hours some other
  way (phone call, in person, etc). The student is notified either way.
- **Remind** — re-sends the confirmation email to a slow supervisor.
  (The app also does this automatically now — see "Automatic reminders"
  below — so manual reminders are for anything that needs a nudge sooner.)
- **Edit** — fix a typo or correct the hours/date/department on *any*
  entry, no matter its status.
- **Delete** — permanently remove an entry, e.g. a duplicate or a mistake.
- **+ Log hours on a student's behalf** — for a student who came to your
  office in person, or whose hours you've confirmed outside the app.
  Enter their AUIS email and the details; it's saved as already-approved,
  no supervisor email is sent, and it counts immediately.

## Semesters — opening a new window

Every cycle, before students start submitting:

1. Go to **Semesters** → **+ Add a new semester**.
2. Fill in the **name**, **start/end dates**, and **submission deadline**
   from the announcement email.
3. Leave **"Make this active"** checked, then **Create**.

The previous semester closes automatically; nothing is ever deleted, just
archived. You can **Edit dates** on any semester at any time, even the
active one, if a date needs to shift later.

## Message Students — sending a group email

Go to **Message Students**, pick an audience:

- **Every student who has logged in** — a general announcement
- **Haven't submitted anything yet** — a first nudge
- **Have an entry awaiting confirmation** — chase-up reminder
- **Below the 10-hour minimum so far** — encourage them toward qualifying

Write a subject and message, click **Review & Send**, then confirm. The
app handles the greeting/sign-off formatting — just write the message
itself plainly.

## Automatic reminders (no action needed from you)

Once a semester's deadline is within 14 days, the app automatically
reminds supervisors of pending entries every few days, without you doing
anything. This runs quietly in the background.

## Award tiers (for reference)

| Tier | Hours |
|---|---|
| Regular | 10–25 |
| Bronze | 26–50 |
| Silver | 51–75 |
| Gold | 76–100 |
| Diamond | 101–250 |

If these ever change, ask IT — it's one file in the code, a five-minute
change.

## A word on supervisor emails

If a supervisor confirmation email doesn't seem to be arriving for
someone, check with IT first — there's a one-time setup step (verifying
the sending domain) that has to be done before emails can reach *any*
real @auis.edu.krd address. Once that's done, it's a permanent fix and
you'll never need to think about it again.

## Adding another admin

If someone else on your team needs this same access, ask IT — it's a
one-line change on their end, not something you can do from inside the
app itself, for security reasons.

## Note on GPA-based awards

The Dean's/President's Award (GPA-based) is **not** tracked in this app,
since the original email states no student action is needed for those.
If you'd like those tracked here too, let IT know.
